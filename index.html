Support for impact founders

Building for impact is meaningful work
— and you don't have to do it alone.

Founders First is a network of practitioners from the impact sector who listen closely, speak honestly, and hold space for founders to think out loud — so the people building something meaningful have somewhere to turn.

Let's be upfront

We are not an incubator or an accelerator.

There's no curriculum, no cohort to keep pace with, no pitch to polish. Just a room where you can say the true thing — the doubt, the question you haven't said out loud yet — to people who understand this work.

What we offer

Whether you're navigating growth, fundraising, team challenges, burnout, strategy questions, or simply the weight of building something meaningful — this is a space to think honestly, without performance.

Listening sessions

One-on-one conversations where you can speak freely about challenges, dilemmas, and uncertainties — confidential, unhurried, and free of judgment.

Perspective & guidance

Experience drawn from across the social impact ecosystem, offered to help you see a challenge from a few more angles and find a practical way through it.

Peer community

A growing circle of social entrepreneurs and ecosystem practitioners who support one another through honest conversation, shared learning, and solidarity.

Founder wellbeing

We believe strong impact starts with a supported founder. Reflection and sustainability aren't a side note here — they're the point.

Who this is for

Founders and leaders building, at any stage from early to scaling:

  • Social enterprises
  • Nonprofits
  • Community-led initiatives
  • Inclusive businesses
  • Mission-driven organizations

Our philosophy

Too often, founders carry the emotional and strategic weight of impact work alone. We believe founders deserve:

  • Spaces to be heard without judgment
  • Trusted peers and practitioners to think alongside them
  • Honest conversation over performative optimism
  • Support that values impact and wellbeing in equal measure
Sustainable impact requires sustainable founders.
