Venture Support for Impact Founders

Building for impact is hard work .. you don't have to do it alone.

Founders First provides you a safe space to think out loud, paired with hands-on venture coaching to help you act. We work alongside entrepreneurs at every step — from the big strategic calls to the hard operational days — so you have both a place to be heard and real help getting things done.

Let's be upfront

We are not an incubator or an accelerator.

There's no curriculum, no cohort to keep pace with, no pitch to polish. Just a room where you can say the true thing — the doubt, the question you haven't said out loud yet — to people who understand this work.

What we offer

Whether you're navigating growth, fundraising, team challenges, burnout, or the weight of building something meaningful — this is hands-on support, at every step — a process that lets you show up authentically, without pretense.

Venture coaching

Hands-on coaching held inside a confidential, safe space — where you can speak freely about challenges, dilemmas, and uncertainties, free of judgment, with nothing to prove.

Strategic support

Hands-on help with the big decisions — fundraising, growth strategy, team structure, positioning — worked through with you, not just advised on from a distance.

Operational support

Real help getting through the hard days — hiring, cash crunches, difficult conversations — with experts who've been in the operational trenches themselves.

Peer community

A growing circle of social entrepreneurs and ecosystem practitioners who support one another through honest conversation, shared learning, and solidarity.

Who this is for

Founders and leaders building, at any stage from early to scaling:

  • Startups in Impact Sectors
  • Social enterprises
  • Nonprofits
  • Community-led initiatives
  • Inclusive businesses
  • Mission-driven organizations
